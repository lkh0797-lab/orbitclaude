## 1) Decisions

- **Font.** Pretendard Variable becomes the single UI font, loaded as a dynamic subset from jsDelivr. It becomes `--sans` and replaces the plates' `--f-body`. Noto Serif KR becomes `--serif`, used for plate headlines and now also for chrome page titles, so the chrome and the plates share one display voice. IBM Plex Mono stays only for plate numerals and Latin eyebrows, and gains the 700 weight it lacks. IBM Plex Sans KR is dropped. Offline, the stack falls back to Segoe UI / Malgun Gothic exactly as today.
- **Type scale.** Eight steps: `--fs-1`…`--fs-8` = 10/11/12/13/14/16/19/24px. One scripted pass replaces the literals; a dry run on the current file made **1,071** replacements.
  - Left untouched: 8px and 9px (data-mark labels), 20px and up (plate display sizes), every rule containing `fill:` or `clamp(` (380 SVG-text and display rules), and all sizes set from JS.
  - Two pins: `table.tbl` at 12px, and KPI numerals at 19px.
- **Numbers.** Tabular figures are forced on everything in `main` and `aside`. This needs `!important` because the `font:` shorthand resets `font-variant-numeric`. Chrome numeric cells move from Consolas to Pretendard, so digits and their 조/억 units use one font. Plates keep Plex Mono.
- **Color.** One warm neutral family in both themes:
  - Light: `--bg #f6f4f0`, `--dim #5a5650`, `--faint #736f68`. Faint text goes from 2.9:1 to 4.55:1.
  - Dark: surfaces move from a green tint (hue ~100°) to warm (~40°), and `--faint` becomes `#928e86`.
  - New state tokens (`--up-bg/--dn-bg/--gold/--on-solid/--on-heat/--line-ctl`) replace raw hex in chrome only. Chart colors are unchanged.
- **Elevation and radius.** Three warm shadows; in dark mode they become a 1px ring plus black. Five radii: 4/6/10/14/pill. Plates go from an 18px radius to 14px and get a hairline ring, so their edge stays visible in dark mode.
- **Frame.**
  - Header is 52px. The search box starts exactly at the content column (x=288). 스크리닝 sits next to the search box; the maintenance buttons move to the right.
  - Main area padding is 20/24/48px; content is capped at 1480px.
  - Both tab rows fit on two lines at 1280px and wider through sizing alone. The DOM stays flat, because JS walks `#tabs.children` at 3807/13752/17884/17896.
- **States.** The sidebar selection becomes a soft accent with a 3px bar instead of a solid slab. One global `:focus-visible` ring (gold on plates). 160ms transitions, 1px press feedback, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` block.
- **Not in Phase A:**
  - Sticky tabs need one JS line.
  - The `#tabs[hidden]` fix needs a markup edit.
  - The radius pass would be a second regex, and only one is allowed.
  - Card padding normalization is medium risk.
  - Per-tab layout work goes to Phase B.
